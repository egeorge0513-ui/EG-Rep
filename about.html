Before every wedding, I sit down with couples to actually learn who they are: how they met, the inside jokes, the moment they knew. That's what ends up in the script.

How I Work

What you can expect

  1. 01We talk first. A planning consultation to hear your story and what you want the ceremony to feel like.
  2. 02I write your script. A draft built around your relationship, shared for your feedback and edits.
  3. 03We finalize details. Timing, readings, vows, and any traditions you want included.
  4. 04I show up and deliver it. Calm, prepared, and focused on making your ceremony feel effortless.
